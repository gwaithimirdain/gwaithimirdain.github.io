Gwaith-i-Mírdain, named for the brotherhood of Jewel-smiths in Tolkien's legendarium who created the rings of power, is our github organization for developing higher-dimensional proof assistants.

Currently the only developer is [Mike Shulman](https://home.sandiego.edu/~shulman/), but new contributors are welcome!
