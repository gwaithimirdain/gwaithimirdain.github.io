I've been accumulating a lot of small ideas and to-dos on Narya for a while, mostly as notes to myself.  Today I took a bunch of those and made them into [Github issues](https://github.com/gwaithimirdain/narya/issues), for the benefit of anyone else who'd like to contribute to Narya and wants a place to get started.  There isn't yet really a good "design document" or introduction to the Narya code, but I'm happy to talk with any potential contributors and answer questions.
